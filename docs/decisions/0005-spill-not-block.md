# ADR 0005 — A full shelf spills; it does not block

**Status:** **superseded by [ADR 0012](0012-output-to-hands.md)** · 2026-09-04 — Sam flagged it, three options were presented, output-to-hands chosen

## Decision
When an output shelf is full, the mini-game still completes and the product lands on the
floor ring. Only a full floor ring jams the machine.

## Why
If the machine simply refuses, the *game* told the player to stop. If the keg rolls onto the
floor behind them, the *player* has to decide to stop — and the pile is visible to everyone
else, which is what starts the conversation. Spilling is also funnier, and hauling floor
kegs is the first Tier D chore players meet.

## Rejected
- **Hard block at shelf capacity** (the handoff's default). Correct, tidy, teaches nothing.
