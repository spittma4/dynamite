# 09 — Progression

Each level is a config file that turns on stations, sets curves and buffers, and picks an
order mix and event weights. Each level is designed to make players **say one specific thing
to each other**. That sentence is the level's acceptance test.

| Level | Name | Stations | Designed to make players say | Milestone |
|-------|------|----------|------------------------------|-----------|
| 1 | Basic Flow | Powder · Assembly · Dock | *"Oh, I bring the keg over here."* | M1 |
| 2 | Too Much Powder | same, Powder 4× Assembly | **"Stop making powder."** | M1 |
| 3 | The Bottleneck | same, 6 players | *"Come help Assembly."* | M1 |
| 4 | Too Many Helpers | same, Assembly curve punishes 4+ | **"No, not everyone."** | M1 |
| 5 | Quality | + Casing Prep · QA · Rework | *"Who's making dented casings?"* | M2 |
| 6 | The Truck | + Packing, crates, Returns | *"Someone stage crates BEFORE the horn."* | M2 |
| 7 | Bad Requirements | ambiguous orders, Phone | *"Just call them."* / *"Just guess."* | M3 |
| 8 | Interruptions | Rush, Change of Mind, Standup, Tour | *"Drop the gold, they cancelled."* | M3 |
| 9 | Management Optimizes Utilization | Per-player "efficiency" HUD that praises busy-ness; bonus budget for high utilization | *"Ignore the number. Ignore the number."* | M4 |
| 10 | Continuous Flow | Everything, all upgrades available | *(silence, then)* *"…did we just do that?"* | M4 |

A level is a **day**. Grade **C or better** unlocks the next; D or F repeats the day
(*"MANAGEMENT WOULD LIKE TO SEE IMPROVEMENT"*). See [18](18-days-grades-and-payday.md).

**Sandbox** — any level, no timer, no grade, debug overlay on, all upgrades free. For tuning and for
playing with friends who just want to make a mess.

## Level 9 deserves a note

The HUD shows each player a big green number: their utilization. It ticks up while you work.
It says EXCELLENT at 90%. The round's budget bonus is tied to the *team average* utilization.
Every incentive in the level says "stay busy". The level is only winnable by ignoring it.

This is the one place the game openly parodies management. It's late enough that players
have earned the joke.
